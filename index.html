FAQ

Questions before you buy

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The kits are designed for non-technical users using common tools like Gmail, Google Sheets, ChatGPT, Zapier, Make, Airtable, and Notion.

Do I need to talk to anyone?

No. AutomationHive is self-serve. You buy the kit, download the files, and follow the setup guide.

Are these custom automations?

No. These are ready-made templates, prompts, and guides. They are designed to be adapted by the buyer.

What happens after I buy?

You receive access to the downloadable files and setup instructions through the checkout platform.

Will these work with every tool my business uses?

The kits are built around common tools and flexible workflows. Buyers are responsible for adapting them to their own systems, permissions, and business rules.

Can I share a kit with my whole team?

The standard license allows use inside one business or team. Redistribution, resale, or repackaging is not permitted.
